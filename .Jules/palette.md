## 2025-01-24 - Semantic Contact Links & Keyboard Navigation
**Learning:** Even high-end, visual-heavy sites often overlook basic accessibility fundamentals like interactive contact links and skip links. Implementing these provides a massive UX win for both mobile users (click-to-call) and power users (keyboard navigation) without compromising the design.
**Action:** Always check for plain-text phone numbers and missing skip links on landing pages with large headers or animations.
