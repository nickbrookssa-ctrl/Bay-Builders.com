## 2025-05-15 - Improving Navigation in Scroll-Heavy Landers

**Learning:** Large scroll heights (500vh+) used for cinematic animations can disorient users if no navigational affordance is provided. Standard scrollbars are often insufficient or customized to the point of invisibility in high-end design.

**Action:** Implement a high-visibility, branded progress bar tied to the scroll state to provide immediate feedback on the user's journey through the animation sequence. This provides a clear sense of place without cluttering the aesthetic.
