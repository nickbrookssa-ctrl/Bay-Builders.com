## 2024-11-15 - High-Asset Scroll Preloading & Screen Reader Hygiene
**Learning:** Dense image-sequence scroll animations (over 200 frames) create a silent "dead zone" of blank layout during initial page load, which frustrates users on slower connections. Implementing a lightweight, ARIA-compliant progress bar matching the brand's primary accent (#ff5a1f) offers immediate, intuitive loading feedback. To prevent race conditions in fast cache environments, image preloading Promises must register handlers (onload, onerror, onabort) *before* assigning source URLs, and complete DOM removal (via element.remove()) must be synchronized with CSS transitions to maintain DOM hygiene and satisfy accessibility tree assertions.
**Action:** Always wrap heavy asset loops with an inclusive tracker (e.g., '>=' check) and resolve even on errors/aborts to prevent infinite loader hangs, then fully purge the loader element from the DOM after fading it out.
