## 2025-05-14 - Focused Micro-UX vs. Unauthorized Redesign
**Learning:** Large-scale redesigns or rebranding (e.g., changing the app name and visual identity) violate the "Palette" mission of providing subtle, non-intrusive UX improvements. Such changes often exceed the 50-line limit and disrupt the existing brand identity.
**Action:** Always stick to incremental, high-impact accessibility and usability improvements (like skip links, ARIA labels, and interactive contact methods) that respect the original design and brand. Verify that all changes are under 50 lines per file.
